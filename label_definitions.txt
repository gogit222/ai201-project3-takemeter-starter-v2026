analysis: Makes an interpretive or evaluative claim about a film and supports it with a specific, checkable detail or comparison that does real work in the reasoning.
hot_take: States a confident general verdict or interpretation about a film or filmmaker without a specific, load-bearing reason; a detail mentioned in passing does not count as support.
reaction: Primarily reports the writer's personal viewing response or taste, rather than using evidence to establish a broader interpretation or claim.
question: Primarily asks for an explanation, recommendation, or interpretation and does not present the answer as settled; supporting details provide context for the question.
